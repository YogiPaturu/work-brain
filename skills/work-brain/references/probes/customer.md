# Customer Probe

## Notice

Stated request, underlying need, direct evidence, surprises, pattern versus
anecdote, and decision impact.

## Conditional probes

- **Customer statement:** Separate the exact signal from the user's
  interpretation and ask what decision it changed.
- **Repeated feedback:** Ask how many observations support the pattern, from
  which segment, and what counter-signal exists.
- **Discovery outcome:** Ask what the user expected to learn and what remains
  uncertain after the conversation.

## Constraint

Preserve the customer's words as evidence without turning one comment into a
general market claim.
