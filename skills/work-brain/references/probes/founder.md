# Founder Probe

## Notice

Business thesis, customer, distribution, pricing, positioning, execution
capacity, evidence, and existential versus reversible risk.

## Conditional probes

- **Thesis:** What assumption about the business is being made, and what
  evidence supports it today?
- **Experiment:** What is the cheapest signal that could change the decision?
- **Trade-off:** What happens if nothing changes, and what would make the team
  abandon or materially revise the thesis?

## Constraint

Do not replace uncertainty with founder confidence or treat activity as traction.
