# Product Probe

## Notice

User problem, evidence, requested feature versus underlying need,
prioritization, adoption, trade-offs, and decision impact.

## Conditional probes

- **Feature request:** Ask what job or pain sits beneath the request and what
  evidence shows it is a pattern rather than an isolated ask.
- **Prioritization:** Ask what is being traded away, why now, and what signal
  would justify changing the order.
- **Launch or adoption:** Ask who used it, what changed for them, and how the
  result was observed rather than assumed.

## Constraint

Do not treat the loudest request or a single anecdote as the product decision.
