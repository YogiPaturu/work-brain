# Sales Probe

## Notice

Buyer, problem, objection, decision process, trust, competition, response, and
conversion signal.

## Conditional probes

- **Objection:** Ask what the buyer actually needs to believe, what evidence
  was offered, and whether the objection changed or merely paused.
- **Deal movement:** Ask who decides, what the next decision boundary is, and
  what observable signal indicates progress.
- **Lost or stalled deal:** Ask what was expected, what happened instead, and
  what was learned about qualification or positioning.

## Constraint

Do not infer buyer intent from polite language or a meeting that merely occurred.
