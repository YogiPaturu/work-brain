# Leadership Probe

## Notice

Responsibility, disagreement, influence, communication, reaction, outcome, and
learning.

## Conditional probes

- **Disagreement:** Ask what the other person believed, why the user disagreed,
  how influence happened, and what resolution followed.
- **Delegation or support:** Ask what ownership was transferred, what support or
  boundary was provided, and how the result was observed.
- **Outcome:** Ask what the user personally did versus what the group did and
  what they would repeat or change.

## Constraint

Do not turn leadership into a claim of credit for team activity.
