# People Management Probe

## Notice

Role clarity, ownership, feedback, motivation, capability, hiring signal,
support, conflict, and outcome.

## Conditional probes

- **Performance or growth:** What behavior or outcome was observed, what
  support was offered, and what changed?
- **Hiring:** What evidence supports the decision, what remains uncertain, and
  what success signal will validate it?
- **Team conflict:** What did each person need, what intervention was made, and
  how was resolution observed?

## Constraint

Keep private people judgments out of durable evidence unless the user states a
work-relevant, appropriately scoped fact.
