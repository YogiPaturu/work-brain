# Strategy Probe

## Notice

Thesis, assumptions, alternatives, downside, reversibility, opportunity cost,
and expected signal.

## Conditional probes

- **Strategic choice:** Ask what decision is actually being made and what is
  being deferred or abandoned.
- **Thesis:** Ask what evidence supports it, what the cheapest useful signal is,
  and what would make the team abandon or revise it.
- **Do nothing:** Ask what happens if nothing changes and whether that risk is
  reversible.

## Constraint

Keep a strategic hypothesis visibly separate from evidence and conclusion.
