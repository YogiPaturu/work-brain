# Marketing Probe

## Notice

Audience, positioning, channel, message, hypothesis, experiment, and signal.

## Conditional probes

- **Campaign or launch:** Ask which audience and behavior were targeted and
  what signal would count as success.
- **Positioning decision:** Ask which alternative message was rejected and what
  evidence supports the chosen framing.
- **Experiment result:** Compare expected and actual signal, including whether
  the test was capable of distinguishing the hypothesis.

## Constraint

Keep reach, engagement, and conversion distinct; do not call attention a result.
