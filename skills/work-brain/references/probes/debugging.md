# Debugging Probe

## Notice

Symptoms, scope, hypotheses, evidence, experiments, narrowing path, root cause,
impact, and recurrence prevention.

## Conditional probes

- **Incident or failure:** Separate observed symptom from interpretation; ask
  what changed, who or what was affected, and how impact was measured.
- **Investigation:** Ask which hypothesis the next experiment distinguishes and
  what result would rule it out.
- **Fix completed:** Ask what evidence confirms the root cause and what
  prevention or monitoring was added.

## Constraint

Prefer the next discriminating question over a chronology of every debugging step.
