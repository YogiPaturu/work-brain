# Engineering Probe

## Notice

Requirements, constraints, architecture, scale, consistency, reliability,
security, operational complexity, alternatives, and reversibility.

## Conditional probes

- **Architecture or design:** Ask which constraint drives the choice, which
  alternatives were serious, what failure mode matters most, and what signal
  would trigger a revisit.
- **Shipped change:** Ask what the user personally owned, what trade-off was
  accepted, and what result or operational signal should change the view.
- **Migration or rollout:** Ask where cutover and rollback begin and end, and
  what unsafe intermediate state must be prevented.

## Constraint

Choose the single highest-value missing question. Do not run this as a checklist.
